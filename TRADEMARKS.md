## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your work uses, is based on, or is compatible with Scriptavera.
- Redistribute **unchanged** copies of Scriptavera under its own name, with the logo, as long as you do not suggest
  that you are the maker or that the maker endorses you.
- Use the name in articles, reviews, papers and teaching material that discuss Scriptavera.

## What needs written permission

- Using the name or the logo for a **changed** version (a fork), or for a product or service of your own. A fork
  must have a different name and logo, and remove the logo files. You may describe it as "based on Scriptavera".
- Offering a hosted service under the name Scriptavera, or under a confusingly similar name or domain.
- Using the logo in a way that suggests a partnership or endorsement.
