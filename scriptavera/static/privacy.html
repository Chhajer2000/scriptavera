Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 • Last updated: November 17, 2026

Scriptavera (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the web application located at https://scriptavera.com. Scriptavera is an academic mathematical authoring and verification platform designed to assist researchers in transforming handwritten research notes into verified LaTeX manuscripts and reproducible scholarly publications.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the intellectual property and personal data of mathematicians and scholars who use our service. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you access or use Scriptavera.

1. Ownership of Mathematical Work

We hold an absolute commitment to mathematical ownership: You retain one hundred percent ownership, copyright, and intellectual property rights over all mathematical notes, photographs, handwritten transcriptions, theorem statements, proofs, and compiled manuscripts you upload to or generate within Scriptavera.

Scriptavera claims zero intellectual property rights over your mathematical research. We do not use your private mathematical notes or unpublished manuscripts to train public foundation models without your explicit, voluntary opt-in.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only to the extent necessary to provide and operate the Scriptavera platform:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-in, we receive your email address, full name, and profile photo URL. We use this information solely to identify your account, secure your research projects, and enforce project privacy.
  • Research Content: Photos of handwritten mathematical notes, transcribed LaTeX snippets, mathematical statements, proof steps, outline structures, and bibliography selections that you submit to your projects.
  • Operational and Technical Data: Transient logs containing IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, session identifiers, timestamps, and error traces necessary to maintain network security, verify session state, and diagnose computational failures.

3. Google API Data and Google Drive Access

Scriptavera provides an optional integration with Google Drive to allow authors to archive their research and receive verified cryptographic proof bundles directly in their cloud storage.

Requested Google OAuth Scope

Scriptavera requests only the following non-restricted, per-file Google Drive scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (View and manage Google Drive files and folders that you have opened or created with this app).

How We Use Google Drive Data

  • Creation of Dedicated Archive Folder: When you connect Google Drive, Scriptavera creates a dedicated folder titled “Scriptavera Papers” in your Google Drive.
  • Direct Document Export: Scriptavera saves your compiled LaTeX source packages, camera-ready PDF outputs, figures, and cryptographic timestamp certificates into that folder upon your explicit command.
  • No Broader File Access: Scriptavera does not read, modify, scan, or access any other personal files, folders, spreadsheets, or documents in your Google Drive. We only interact with files created directly by Scriptavera or specifically selected by you.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
Scriptavera’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Artificial Intelligence and Language Model Processing

To assist in transcribing mathematical handwriting, planning section outlines, auditing proofs for logical gaps, and copy-editing mathematical English, Scriptavera interfaces with specialized AI model endpoints (such as Google Gemini and high-throughput inference APIs):

  • Transcribed passages and proof steps are transmitted securely via encrypted API channels solely to generate the requested mathematical assistance.
  • Model API interactions adhere to commercial and developer data protection standards that do not retain prompt data for generalized public model training.
  • In self-hosted and private desktop configurations, users may provide their own API keys, ensuring direct control over their model provider agreements.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or research data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks. We share information only under the following limited conditions:

  • Infrastructure Providers: With trusted infrastructure providers (such as Google Cloud hosting and database services) necessary to host and deliver the web service securely.
  • Legal Compliance: If strictly required by applicable law, regulation, subpoena, or lawful governmental request.

6. Data Security and Encryption

We implement industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to protect your mathematical drafts:

  • All traffic between your browser and Scriptavera is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Project files on the server are isolated using strict multi-tenant access controls so that no author can view or access another author's manuscripts.

7. Data Retention and User Control

  • Project Deletion: You may delete any project or paper draft from your dashboard at any time. When a project is deleted, its draft files, images, and notes are removed from active servers.
  • Disconnecting Google Drive: You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from your Account settings, or revoke Scriptavera’s access entirely via your Google Account Permissions page.

8. Children’s Privacy

Scriptavera is intended for university students, academic researchers, and professional mathematicians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 16.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal updates. Any changes will be posted directly to this URL with an updated effective date.
